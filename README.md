# Andreas Krassnigg

Theoretical physicist in Graz, Austria — PhD in few-body and hadron physics, Privatdozent at the University of Graz
since 2010 — working on applied AI since 2020: four years at [Innophore](https://innophore.com), most recently as Head
of AI and Data Science. Today I run **[Krassnigg Digital](https://github.com/krassniggdigital)**: AI consulting,
workshops and AI video for small businesses, associations and the self-employed.
